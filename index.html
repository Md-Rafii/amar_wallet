How it works

1. Choose a template
Search by occasion, style or photo count.
2. Add your photos
Select many at once; they fill the slots in order.
3. Customize
Text (Bengali too), colors, stickers, filters.
4. Download & print
PNG, JPG or a true A4 PDF.

Free vs PRO

PRO badges and the watermark are driven by a config object in the code (CFG), ready to be connected to an admin panel and payments later. Right now every template is usable and free exports carry a small watermark.

FAQ

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser; designs are saved on this device (IndexedDB).

Can I write in Bangla?

Yes. Bengali fonts (Hind Siliguri, Noto Serif Bengali) are built in. Keep letter spacing at 0 for Bengali so letters stay joined.
